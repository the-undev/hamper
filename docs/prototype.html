Hamper

A clickable sketch of the four screens. Everything you tap works on the sample data and resets on reload. Nothing here is final design; it is for checking the flow.

Plan

  • Meals one slot per day from the start date. Tap an empty slot and pick a meal, or type anything ("Takeaway", "Leftovers and garlic bread") for a day that is not a library meal. Placing a meal copies its lines onto the day.
  • Day tap a planned meal's name to open the day: change its items for that day only, Reset to the library meal, or Save as a meal for an ad-hoc day. The library meal never changes from here.
  • Press the ≡ handle and drag onto another day to swap, finger or mouse. Swipe a meal left to clear the day. The date in the header opens a date picker for the start; − + beside it sets the number of days. The slots relabel and the meals stay put. "Start new plan" at the bottom moves the date on and clears Once items, after a confirm; nothing happens to the plan on its own.
  • Items type in the bar: matches come from items used before, the first row creates a new one. Each line has a count. "Weekly" marks an item that stays on the plan after a shop is archived; the rest clear. Swipe a line left to remove it.

Shop

  • "Make from plan" first shows the breakdown: every day's lines and the wanted list, editable, saved to the plan. Generate then sums counts per item, each line saying where it came from. "Start empty" is for a mid-week trip; type the lines in.
  • More than one list can be open. The row at the top switches between them.
  • Tap a line to change its name, size or count, put it on wanted as Once ("Out of stock"), or remove it. The plan does not change.
  • Tick as you go. Ticked lines drop to the bottom.
  • Swipe a line left for "To wanted" (puts it on the plan as Once) or "Remove". "Rest to wanted" does that for every unticked line at once.
  • Share shows the text that would go to WhatsApp. Archive moves the list to History, Delete drops it. Neither touches the plan.

Meals

The library. Open a meal to change its lines or add it to the next empty slot. The camera button stands in for photo, crop, upload.

More

Items (rename, merge, usual size), History with "copy meals to plan", export and import, and the offline switch, which here just shows the bar.
